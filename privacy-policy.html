CalorieTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information CalorieTracker ("the app"), offered by Kaplun, collects, how it is used, and your choices about it. Contact: danielkaplun898@gmail.com.

Summary

CalorieTracker is designed to keep your data on your device. Food logs, weight history, progress photos, and your account are stored locally on your phone. We do not sell your data. The only information that ever leaves your device is described below.

Data stored on your device

  • Your account (email and a hashed password). The password is hashed locally and is not readable by us.
  • Food and meal logs, nutrition goals, weight history, water intake, measurements, progress photos, workout and health entries.
  • Preferences such as your trial/premium status and ad consent choice.

Progress photos are stored in the app's private storage on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere.

Data processed when you use a feature

  • Photo Food AI (optional): if you use it, a food photo is sent to Google Gemini, which is operated by Google, only to estimate calories and macros. The photo is not stored by us.
  • Payments: premium purchases are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details.
  • Barcode scanning: looks up product data; only the scanned code is used for the look-up.

Third-party services

  • Google Gemini (Google LLC) - optional AI photo analysis. Information: ai.google.dev.
  • Stripe - secure payment processing. Information: stripe.com/privacy.
  • AdMob (Google LLC) - only if ads are enabled and you have consented. Information: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights & deletion

  • Delete your account and all entered data in the app under Settings (account deletion).
  • Ask us to delete any data at any time by emailing danielkaplun898@gmail.com.
  • Contact us to correct or export your data or ask questions about this policy.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
