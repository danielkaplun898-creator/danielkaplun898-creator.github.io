## Sourcing tips

- Message 4-6 suppliers, not just one. Compare MOQ, unit price, sample cost, lead time.
- On Alibaba always use **Trade Assurance** (buyer protection) and pay deposit via secure channel.
- Samples first: order 2-3 units before committing to bulk.
- Price guide: $5-15/unit at MOQ 100-500 depending on chipset (BT 5.0 cheapest, 5.4/ANC more expensive).
- Verify claims: ask for a factory video call and send your spec table if they say "we have a matching model".
