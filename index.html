CalorieTracker
A comprehensive nutrition tracking app built with Flutter. Search 300,000+ foods, log meals, scan barcodes, and hit your fitness goals.

Track Every Bite.

CalorieTracker makes it effortless to monitor your daily nutrition. Powered by the USDA FoodData Central database with real nutritional data for hundreds of thousands of foods.

  • ✓ Search 300,000+ foods with USDA data
  • ✓ Log meals by breakfast, lunch, dinner & snack
  • ✓ Scan barcodes for instant food lookup
  • ✓ Track protein, carbs, fat & calories
  • ✓ Weekly history & statistics with charts
  • ✓ Cloud sync with user accounts
  • ✓ Customizable daily calorie & macro goals

Hi, I'm Kaplun - an independent app developer focused on building clean, functional mobile applications. I believe in creating tools that solve real problems and make everyday life a little easier.


My first release, CalorieTracker, is a comprehensive nutrition tracking app built with Flutter. It features food search powered by the USDA database, barcode scanning, meal logging, macro tracking, and cloud sync - everything you need to stay on top of your nutrition goals.
