Features
Everything you need
A complete nutrition tracking toolkit in your pocket.
🔍

USDA Food Search

Search 600,000+ foods powered by the USDA FoodData Central database. Real nutritional data for real foods.

📱

Barcode Scanner

Scan product barcodes for instant nutrition lookup. 1M+ products in the database. Pro feature with 7-day free trial.

🍽️

Meal Logging

Log breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks separately. See exactly where your calories come from.

📊

Macro Tracking

Track protein, carbs, fat, and fiber with color-coded progress. Auto-calculated goals based on your stats.

🏆

Achievements

12 unlockable badges for milestones. Stay motivated with streaks, protein goals, and consistency rewards.

🍕

Custom Foods

Create your own food entries for homemade meals and recipes. Full nutrition breakdown included.

💧

Water Tracking

Track daily water intake with a simple tap. Set goals and monitor your hydration progress.

⚖️

Weight Tracker

Log weight over time with interactive line charts. Visualize your progress toward your goal weight.

📐

Body Measurements

Track waist, chest, arms, and hips with trend charts. See how your body composition changes.

🧮

BMI & TDEE Calculator

Auto-calculated BMI with categories, and Mifflin-St Jeor equation for your personalized calorie goal.

📈

Statistics & Charts

Weekly bar charts, macro pie charts, daily averages, and comprehensive history with search.

📤

CSV Export

Export all your meal data to CSV. Import into spreadsheets or share with your nutritionist.
