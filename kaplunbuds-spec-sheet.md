## 1. Product Overview

Semi-in-ear/in-ear TWS earphones with charging case. Device connects via Bluetooth,
supports touch control, dual-microphone ENC for calls. Supplied with charging case,
ear tips (S/M/L) and USB-C cable. PCB firmware provided by factory or by customer IO.

## 10. Quality / Sampling (Required by Buyer)

- **IQC**: incoming component inspection records
- **Sampling (FAA)**: 3 pcs pilot sample before mass production; approve logo, colors, packaging
- **IPQC/OQC**: inline + final QC; 100% functional (pairing, charging, controls, audio) sampling per AQL 2.5
- **Tests**: charge cycles >; drop test records; water-resist test; Bluetooth pairing stability
- **Yield**: >= 97% per production batch; defective units replaced FOC within warranty
- Warranty: 12 months from shipment
